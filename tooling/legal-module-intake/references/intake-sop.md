# Local-to-library SOP

## Lane A: private matter package

Keep the full internal record: file inventory, facts by neutral identifiers, issue map, chronology, evidence map, research trail, decisions, work product, reviewer notes, and the relationship between outputs and source files. This lane may contain protected data and remains local or in the firm's approved private system.

## Lane B: public module candidate

Extract only a reusable capability. Replace matter facts with typed inputs or synthetic examples. Retain public authorities with source dates. State what the module cannot decide and when a responsible lawyer must intervene.

Do not "anonymize" by merely removing names. Combination risk, rare dates, project names, amounts, locations, procedural posture, quotations, document metadata, and distinctive fact sequences can identify a matter.

## Required gates

1. **Authority:** the contributor has authority to reuse and disclose the material.
2. **Confidentiality and privilege:** no protected information or privileged reasoning is disclosed or inferable.
3. **Third-party rights:** copyrighted, licensed, database, platform, and personal-data restrictions are respected.
4. **Generalization:** the candidate expresses a method or capability, not the client's outcome or hidden facts.
5. **Source integrity:** public sources are cited, dated, and separated from contributor assertions.
6. **Boundary:** `use_when`, `not_for`, inputs, outputs, stopping and escalation conditions are explicit.
7. **Validation:** schema, source, peer, maintainer, field, and matter-suitability states are not collapsed into one badge.
8. **Responsible approval:** a named responsible lawyer approves the exact candidate version for publication.

Any failed or unknown gate means `HOLD_PRIVATE`. The remedy is review or redrafting, not reducing the warning label.
